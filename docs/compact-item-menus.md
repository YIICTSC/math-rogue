# Compact adventure inventories

Item collections use square icon cells with short names and quantity, equipment or lock badges. Tap, keyboard activation, or a stationary 450 ms hold selects a cell and shows its full description. Using, equipping, buying, crafting or transferring requires a separate action in the inspector. Movement over 10 px or a cancelled pointer cancels the hold; releasing a hold suppresses its trailing click. Cells remain scrollable and at least 44 px across.

- Dungeons 1 / 2: combat inventory and equipment, dungeon shops, town shops, warehouses, containers and notebook supplies/equipment. Existing game rules still control identification, curse restrictions, capacity, prices and turn costs. Up/down retains item selection and left/right highlights the inventory action. Escape closes the inventory and restores focus.
- Dungeons 1: a Techniques notebook tab and the existing quick technique menu share the same compact browser. Eleven weapon types include unarmed techniques; inspecting a learned or locked technique shows its power, area, usage rank and discovery requirements. Only Select technique changes the active technique. Tips are collapsible, and inspecting a technique scrolls its details into view.
- Star Tide: one cell per weapon, with A/B equipment badges and separate equip actions; food, ingredients, recipes and upgrades use the same inspector. Portrait places the collection above the Nagi/detail area; landscape places them side by side. Study timing and combat are unchanged.
- RPG: crafting, furniture stock, materials, fishing collection, farm item cards and builder workshop materials/recipes/storage use the shared inspector. Native forms, budget controls and live gathering actions retain their layout. The adapter invokes each original React action from its inspector, preserving server authority, disabled conditions and ambient music.

Browser checks: `scripts/test-dungeons-notebook-browser.mjs`, `scripts/test-star-tide-actions-browser.mjs`, `scripts/test-rpg-menu-selection-browser.mjs`, `scripts/test-rpg-workshop-menu-browser.mjs`. These cover responsive layouts, tap/hold without consumption, explicit actions, equipment persistence, container turn costs, shop/warehouse transfers, technique selection and menu music inheritance.
