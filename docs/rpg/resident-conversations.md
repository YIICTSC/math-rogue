# Resident conversations and outings

- 24 authored daily conversation topics, 24 personality replies, relationship-dependent closing lines, seasonal openings, and occasional playful misunderstandings. Learned words remain supported and alternate with daily topics. Per-bond counters rotate the dialogue and are saved.
- 12 short, interactive resident stories, each with three choices, personality responses, friendship effects, and recorded memories. Each resident/player pair sees all twelve before repeating; the existing conversation cooldown also applies to starting a story.
- 12 flower reactions and eight each for favorite, neutral, and disliked food. Four-line gift exchanges include personalized follow-up responses and acknowledgements of special gifts. Disliked food is acknowledged honestly. Existing daily flower limits, dish consumption, preferences, and birthday gift behavior are preserved. Delivered food gifts also use the expanded responses.
- Six outings retain their three-stage shared action/consent flow, with 54 authored route/stage choices. Each action receives a voiced exchange and completion records a memory and the existing reward.
- Six ImageGen backgrounds are used only for actual outings, including their final conversation: blossom picnic, riverside, stargazing ruins, forest, snowfield tea, and dunes. Both hero and resident are displayed. Backgrounds use optimized 512px WebP files (under 0.8 MiB combined), sliced from the generated six-panel atlas. Original generation: `exec-b69856d4-bd03-4533-bdfd-382432bcbc24.png`, retained in the session generated_images directory.
- Outdoor resident conversations show a temporary 3D view of their actual map location. The map preferences remain untouched; exiting restores the original 2D/3D presentation. The underlying map pauses while the conversation scene owns input. The target actor is suppressed in the background to avoid duplicate portraits. Conversation backgrounds render at 15 fps, keeping controls responsive; unavailable WebGL uses the 2D fallback without changing preferences. Indoor conversations retain the room background.

Validation scripts: `test-rpg-resident-conversations.mjs`, `test-rpg-resident-world.mjs`, `test-rpg-resident-browser.mjs`, existing town and lifestyle checks. Browser coverage includes real 3D conversation backgrounds, five viewports, story choices, flower reactions, all six outing backgrounds with both participants, completion, and restoration of original 2D/3D preferences. Web English gate/build and server build are required before publication.

## Dedicated NPC story scenes

Rino the Courier, Phil the Forester, Mio the Ferryman, Saha the Cartographer, Yuki the Lighthouse Keeper, and Towa the Clockmaker now use their existing standing portraits directly on both map renderers. Six free scenarios each have four versions (24 total). A seeded choice is made when the adventure world starts and shared by each NPC/goal pair. Accepted progress records the variant, so dialogue, journal, discoveries and endings remain stable through save/reload. Old saves retain their original version. Each version has an illustrated clue and two endings; existing proximity, busy-state and one-time reward guards remain intact.

The twelve roaming traveler encounters now have an alternate version each (24 total), selected from the expanded biome pool at adventure creation. Their choices retain cost/reward descriptions and existing reward rules.

Both story and traveler scenes are full-screen regions with a permanently available close button, a matching background, portrait, and event illustration. Six themed background assets and 36 clue/encounter illustrations are generated with ImageGen and stored as WebP under `public/sprites/rpg/events/`. Generated sources are retained in `/workspace/generated_images`: background `exec-f0fb167d-c97d-4d9f-89bb-e036c55f31b2.png`; final clue atlas `exec-1d6b6b8d-b03e-4116-ac02-6e595755d8c5.png`; final traveler atlas `exec-5a5b3389-f119-47af-8771-671521612034.png`.
