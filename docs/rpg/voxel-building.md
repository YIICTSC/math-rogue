# RPG 3D block building

Three.js renders authoritative shared voxels using the Blender unit cube in `public/models/storybook/voxel-block.glb`. Rebuild with `blender -b --python scripts/blender/build-voxel-block.py`. Each seamless cube represents approximately one meter; eye height is 1.62 units.

In 3D, the left controls move continuously and the right analog stick smoothly changes yaw and pitch. Both support simultaneous touch pointers. Four persistent configurable hotbar slots and break/place buttons remain at bottom right. Aim with the crosshair before using an action. Canvas taps retain ordinary map interactions. Spectators cannot build.

Basic mining and placement cost 0.1 gathering energy. Stone costs 0.3, brick 0.2, ore 0.6, crystal 0.8 and steel 1.2. Answer questions to recover energy. Craft stone, iron and steel pickaxes to reduce hard-material costs by 25%, 50% and 70%, with a minimum cost of 0.1. Placement also consumes its material. Nineteen materials include soil, sand, snow, steel, leaves, snow-covered leaves, fruit, shrubs, reeds, herbs and cactus alongside the original seven. Natural trees have two trunk blocks and a spreading leaf crown; fruit trees bear a distinct fruit block. Each cell can be harvested and placed independently for 0.1 energy. Cutting a trunk preserves its crown; removing all natural cells clears the 2D resource. Vegetation uses procedural pixel textures, so no extra image downloads are required.

Six biomes have terraced hills and mountains, with ramps near roads and protected locations. Players automatically climb one-block steps; collision prevents passage through higher walls. Ground can be excavated down to unbreakable bedrock at -18. Underground caves contain six seeded oasis chambers, glowing mushrooms and deep steel deposits. Water cannot be mined. Construction reaches layer 18 and worlds allow 18,000 sparse edits. Protected boundaries, NPCs, events, houses, farms and city lots remain intact. Support and 4.5-unit reach are validated by the host/server. Connected overhangs are allowed.

Fractional x/z and feet elevation, discoveries, tools, inventories and voxel edits share the existing save and network infrastructure. Returning to 2D clears the fractional/elevation coordinates and selects the containing walkable tile, or the nearest safe tile when necessary. Existing edited columns retain their previous flat terrain through migration. Entering/leaving houses clears stale 3D coordinates. Peer room protocol is 24.

Validation: `scripts/test-rpg-voxel.mjs` covers terrain, underground chambers, fractional energy, tools, movement, collisions, authority and persistence. `scripts/test-rpg-voxel-server.mjs` uses two real WebSocket clients to verify movement, excavation, placement, inventory and energy synchronization. `scripts/test-rpg-world3d-browser.mjs` exercises actual ray interactions, simultaneous movement/look touches, underground rendering and controls on five portrait, landscape and desktop sizes. `scripts/test-rpg-vegetation-browser.mjs` verifies natural fruit trees, actual ray harvesting/placement and all new hotbar choices. Unit tests also verify all seven vegetation types and their saved inventory/edits. Run the English gate, frontend/server builds and Android asset verification before publication.

Craft a hoe before tilling the single cell ahead using the gathering shortcut. Only tilled cells become farmland; existing planted plots migrate without losing crops. Pets live and wander only inside owned homes. They are managed from the home pet menu, not the outdoor farm panel.

Craft and place a two-block-high door in a space enclosed by walls, floor and a roof (4–256 cells), then register it as a private home or shared facility. Existing furniture and game furniture can be crafted, placed, rotated and packed there. Shared facilities allow other participants to furnish and play games; private homes restrict access. Rooms containing pets cannot switch to shared mode. Broken enclosures reject furniture changes until repaired. Room state, furniture and pets are saved and synchronized.

Insufficient energy on either 3D build button opens the existing learning recovery questions. Underground rendering uses actual voxel surfaces without a ground plane covering excavations, and decorative butterflies are omitted. `scripts/test-rpg-building-life.mjs` checks enclosure validation, permissions, doors, furniture, home-only pets, tilling and save migration.
